Hermes Books Reader

Private & read-only

Hermes Books Reader is a private, personal-use tool that reads its owner's QuickBooks Online company data. It is not a distributed product and has no users other than its owner.

  • Read-only: retrieves transactions, accounts, vendors, and reports; never creates, updates, deletes, or voids anything.
  • Scope-limited: requests only com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting, connected to a single company.
  • Local: runs on the owner's own machine; no cloud backend, no third parties, no tracking.
