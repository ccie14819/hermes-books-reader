Hermes Books Reader — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

Hermes Books Reader ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner. This policy describes how the app handles data. Because the app has exactly one user (its owner), "you" and "the user" refer to that owner.

2. What data the app accesses

Through Intuit's QuickBooks Online API, the app reads accounting data from the owner's own company: transactions, accounts, vendors, customers, and financial reports. The app requests the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting scope only and issues read-only requests — it never writes, creates, deletes, or voids data.

3. Where data is stored

All credentials (OAuth client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens) are stored in the owner's locally-run HashiCorp Vault instance on the owner's own hardware. Retrieved accounting data is processed in memory and summarized locally; no data is uploaded to any third-party service. There is no external database, no cloud backend, and no third-party processor involved in operating the app.

4. Data sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share, or transfer data to any third party. It contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Data is not used to train AI/ML models, whether by the app or any service it uses.

5. Security measures

  • Secrets live only in the local Vault; they are never written to plain-text files, logs, or source control.
  • OAuth refresh tokens rotate on every renewal and are stored with version history.
  • The app enforces read-only access at the transport layer: all non-read requests are blocked before leaving the machine.

6. Data retention

Query results exist in memory during use. Persistent storage consists only of the OAuth credentials in the local Vault, which are retained until the owner disconnects the app or deletes them.

7. Your choices

The owner can disconnect the app at any time via QuickBooks Online → Settings → Apps, revoking all access immediately. The owner can also delete stored credentials from the local Vault at any time.

8. Contact

This app is private; the owner is the only data subject. Intuit reviewer contact information is on file in the developer portal profile associated with this app.

Hermes Books Reader — private, read-only, single-company use.
