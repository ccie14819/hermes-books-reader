Hermes Books Reader — Terms of Service

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. What this is

Hermes Books Reader ("the app") is a private, personal-use software tool built by its owner for the sole purpose of reading the owner's own QuickBooks Online company data. It is not distributed, marketed, sold, or offered to anyone else. There are no other users.

2. What the app does

The app connects to QuickBooks Online via Intuit's OAuth 2.0 API and issues read-only requests (HTTP GET, plus queries to the read-only query endpoint). It retrieves accounting data such as transactions, accounts, vendors, and reports. It does not create, update, delete, or void any data in QuickBooks Online, and it does not initiate payments.

3. Authorization and scope

The app requests only the com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting OAuth scope. Access is limited to the single QuickBooks company that the account owner explicitly authorizes. The owner may disconnect the app at any time from QuickBooks Online's Apps settings, which immediately revokes its access.

4. Data handling

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is processed on the owner's own computer, stored in the owner's locally-run secret management system, and is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. No analytics, advertising, or tracking are included. Data is not used to train any model or external service.
